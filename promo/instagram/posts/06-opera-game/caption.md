## Alt text

Twenty slides retelling the Opera Game, Paul Morphy against the Duke of Brunswick and Count Isouard, Paris 1858, on a cinematic 3D chess board: each slide shows one moment with its moves, from the quiet opening through the knight and rook sacrifices and the queen sacrifice on b8 to the final checkmate by the rook on d8.
