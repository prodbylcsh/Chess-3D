For: primary + Gen Z ranked gamers: the ladder they already understand

## Caption

Chess has ranks now. Iron → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Emerald → Diamond → Master → Grandmaster → Challenger. 🏆

Win +20, lose −18, about five wins per division. Seasons every three months with rewards for the highest rank you reach: coins, an item, a badge and an icon nobody can buy.

Skill only. Every game is checked for engine help, and coins never buy an advantage.

Where would you land? Comment your guess 👇

## Alt text

Twelve slides explaining the Wizard Chess ranked ladder: the rank emblems, shield-shaped crests in the colours of each tier from grey Iron to golden Challenger with a crown; how points are won and lost; the season rewards table; a note on fair play; and the question where you would land.
