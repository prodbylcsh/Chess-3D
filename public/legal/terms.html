5. Timed games

  • Ranked, casual and coin games are played with a clock. The time keeps running if you disconnect or leave, and running out of time loses the game (or draws it, if your opponent can no longer checkmate). Leaving a running game counts as a loss.
  • If a player doesn’t make their first move within 30 seconds, the game is cancelled and changes nothing.

  • In a game for coins, both players’ stakes are set aside when the game starts. The winner receives both stakes minus a 10% fee; a draw or a cancelled game returns them. Games with fewer than 10 moves per player earn no coins in ranked and casual play.
  • After a ranked or casual game, the Stockfish chess engine analyses it on both players’ devices. When both analyses agree, your accuracy adds a small bonus or deduction to that game’s rating and coins (at most a fifth of the base amount); otherwise the game counts as average play.
  • Puzzles come in modules of 100. The first module is free; the others are unlocked with coins and stay unlocked. A puzzle earns coins the first time you solve it, every tenth solved puzzle in a module earns a bonus, and finishing a module gives an item from the shop you don’t own yet, chosen at random (or coins if you own them all). The puzzles come from the Lichess puzzle database (public domain, CC0).
  • Coins, items, rank, statistics and game history are kept on our servers and follow your account. When they moved there (11 October 2026), everyone started fresh: progress kept in browsers by earlier versions of the app was not carried over.
