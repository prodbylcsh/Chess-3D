4. Fair play and behaviour

Chess is only fun when it is fair. Don’t:

  • use chess engines, other people or any other help during your games;
  • manipulate ratings or rewards, for example by playing against your own accounts, losing on purpose or arranging results;
  • exploit bugs (please report them to us instead), or alter the game analysis your browser sends;
  • harass, threaten or insult other players, or share illegal or hateful content;
  • attack or overload the service, access it with bots or automated scripts, or try to get around its protections.

You can block a player (you will no longer be friends, neither of you can send the other a friend request, and you are not matched in games) and report a player or a message to us. We review every report. A report that is knowingly false breaks these rules too.

Other people may watch your running games as your setting allows (Settings → Who can watch my games). Watching is for watching: helping a player during their game breaks these rules. In ranked and coin games, spectators see the moves two moves late while the game runs.

We check ranked, casual and coin games for engine help: how often a player finds the engine’s preferred move, how they spend their time, and whether they leave the game during their moves (Privacy Policy, “Fair play”). Unusual numbers only bring a player to our attention: a person always looks at the games before we act.

If someone breaks these rules, we may cancel game results, reset rank or coins, change an offensive username, or suspend or delete the account. Where reasonable, we tell you why, and you can reply to seblis@seznam.cz.

5. Timed games

  • Ranked, casual and coin games are played with a clock. The time keeps running if you disconnect or leave, and running out of time loses the game (or draws it, if your opponent can no longer checkmate). Leaving a running game counts as a loss.
  • If a player doesn’t make their first move within 30 seconds, the game is cancelled and changes nothing.

6. Coins, items and ranks

  • Coins, items (piece sets, boards, backgrounds, effects, icons), ranks and season rewards are virtual. They have no real-money value: they can’t be exchanged for money or anything outside Wizard Chess, can’t be transferred or sold to other players, and aren’t refunded.
  • You may use them in Wizard Chess for as long as your account exists, but you don’t own them. When an account is deleted, its coins and items are gone.
  • We may change prices, items, rewards and the rules for coins and ranks, and we may correct balances that come from errors or abuse. Ranks change at the end of each season as described in the game.
  • In a game for coins, both players’ stakes are set aside when the game starts. The winner receives both stakes minus a 10% fee; a draw or a cancelled game returns them. Games with fewer than 10 moves per player earn no coins in ranked and casual play.
  • After a ranked, casual or coin game, the Stockfish chess engine analyses it on both players’ devices. In ranked and casual games, when both analyses agree, your accuracy adds a small bonus or deduction to that game’s rating and coins (at most a fifth of the base amount); otherwise the game counts as average play. A coin game pays only its stake; its analysis shows your accuracy.
  • Puzzles come in modules of 100. The Openings module is free; the others are unlocked with coins and stay unlocked, or are open while you have Premium (below). A puzzle earns coins the first time you solve it (hints take up to 10 of them off), every tenth solved puzzle in a module earns a bonus, and finishing a module gives an item from the shop you don’t own yet, chosen at random (or coins if you own them all). Most puzzles come from the Lichess puzzle database (public domain, CC0); the opening lines are our own.
  • A puzzle replayed within 20 hours of your last attempt is practice: your stars can improve only after that. If you look at a puzzle’s solution, it counts as seen: no coins or stars, the next puzzle opens, and solving it later in a review earns its coins and stars then.
  • The daily puzzle earns coins once a day (hints take up to 10 of them off). Solving it on days in a row builds a streak; some streak lengths earn extra coins, and a year and 1000 days also an item from the shop you don’t own yet. A day without the daily puzzle (by UTC time) ends the streak, unless you hold a streak shield for it: a shield is used automatically for each missed day and keeps the streak (the missed day earns nothing). Shields can be bought for coins or come with Premium. The amounts are shown in the app and may change.
  • Learn teaches the game in chapters of lessons. The first two chapters are free; the others open together for coins and stay open, the ones still being written included, or are open while you have Premium. Finishing a chapter earns coins once. The amounts are shown in the app and may change.
  • Without an account you can try the first puzzles of the free module, the daily puzzle and the free chapters of Learn as a guest. What you earn as a guest is kept only for this device and moves into the account you create or sign in to there, by the account’s rules: a puzzle the account has already solved earns nothing again.
  • Coins, items, rank, statistics and game history are kept on our servers and follow your account. When they moved there (11 October 2026), everyone started fresh: progress kept in browsers by earlier versions of the app was not carried over.
