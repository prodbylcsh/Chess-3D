Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Wizard Chess collects, why, who helps us process it, and what rights you have. We keep it short and collect only what the game needs.

1. Who we are

Wizard Chess is run by Lukáš Schöbel, Czech Republic (“we”, “us”). He is the controller of your personal data. Contact: seblis@seznam.cz.

2. What we collect and why

Your account

When you sign up with email: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form), when you signed up and last signed in, and your interface language, so we can write our emails in your language. We use this to create and protect your account, sign you in and send you the emails the account needs (confirming your address, resetting your password, confirming an email change).
Legal basis: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google gives us your email address, name, profile picture link and a Google account ID. We use the email address for your account. The rest is kept with your sign-in record and is not shown in the game.
Legal basis: contract.

Your profile

Your username, profile icon, the date you joined, your progress through the first-time setup and when you last changed your username. Your username, icon and join date are visible to other players, and so are your rank, your game statistics, the awards you have earned (with when and the game that earned them), the items you use and whether you are online right now.
Legal basis: contract.

Friends and messages

Your friends and friend requests, and the messages you send and receive (text and game invitations), with when they were sent and read. Only you and the other player can see a conversation.
Legal basis: contract.

Online games

The games you play online: the moves, the clocks, the result, the names shown at the board, the players’ account IDs and when the game was played. Anyone who has a game’s link can watch it.
Legal basis: contract.

Ranked, casual and coin games, coins and items

Your rating (MMR), coins, the items you own and use (for each item: how you got it, for how many coins, and when), your game statistics and streaks, and your season progress. A record of every change to your coins (games, stakes, payouts, purchases, season rewards). For each ranked, casual or coin game, a result: the opponent, the outcome and how the game ended, the number of moves, and the rating and coins it brought you. After a ranked or casual game, the analysis your browser sends us: a score for every position of the game, worked out on your device by the Stockfish chess engine. We compare it with your opponent’s analysis and, when they match, store your accuracy with the result and adjust that game’s rating and coins by it. While you search for a game, we keep your search (mode, time control, stake and rating) to find you an opponent, and delete it when a game is found or you stop searching.
Your exact rating, your coins and what each game brought you are visible only to you. Other signed-in players can see your recent results (opponent, outcome, how the game ended, number of moves, accuracy). Your username, icon and rank at the time appear in your opponents’ results.
Legal basis: contract.

Coin purchases

When you buy coins: for each purchase, the Paddle transaction number, the pack, the coins, the amount and currency you paid, and whether it was paid or refunded, with the dates. Paddle, which sells the coins (see below), asks for your email address, country and payment details; we never see your card or bank details.
Legal basis: contract; keeping records of purchases is also our legitimate interest in handling refunds and disputes.

Guests

If you play through an invite link without an account, we create an anonymous guest session (a random ID). We store only the name you enter and your games.
Legal basis: contract.

Technical data

Our providers’ servers record your IP address, browser and device information and the time of requests. This keeps the service running and secure (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts).
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you write to us

We use your message and email address to answer you.

3. What stays in your browser

The app keeps in your browser’s storage your sign-in session, your settings (language, sound, camera, time control), the name you last used as a guest, a copy of your profile so it shows quickly, and the moves of your last ranked or casual games until your browser has analysed them (at most a day). Games against the computer and on one device are played only in your browser and are not sent to us.

Since 11 October 2026, your coins, items, rating and game history are kept on our servers (see above). Everyone started fresh then: what earlier versions of the app kept in browsers was not carried over, and we never received it.

We don’t use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. The fonts and the game’s files, including the Stockfish chess engine that analyses your games on your device, are served from our own site; the 3D models and pictures of shop items come from our storage at Cloudflare (see below). Only when you open the payment window to buy coins does your browser load Paddle’s checkout from Paddle’s servers.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA): database, accounts and server functions. Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Resend (USA): sends our emails.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts the website and sees your IP address when you load it.
  • Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare, Inc., USA): stores the 3D models and pictures of shop items and delivers them; sees your IP address when your browser loads them.
  • Google: only if you sign in with Google.
  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited, United Kingdom): sells the coins as our reseller and takes the payment. Paddle receives your account ID and the pack from us, and everything you enter in its checkout, and handles it as an independent controller under its privacy policy.

Supabase, Resend and Cloudflare process data only on our behalf, under data processing terms. GitHub, Google and Paddle handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies. We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are based in the USA. Transfers to them rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Paddle is in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises as protecting personal data adequately.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account removes them right away.
  • Coins, items, rating, statistics, game results and the coin record: until you delete your account, which deletes them right away. Your name in your opponents’ results stays as part of their history.
  • Coin purchases: until you delete your account. Paddle keeps its own records of the payment as the law requires of a seller.
  • Searches for a game: until a game is found or you stop searching.
  • Game analyses (the scores per position): two days; the accuracy stays with the result.
  • Online games: kept so that games can be resumed and looked back on. Games without a registered player are deleted after 6 months without activity. When you delete your account, your games are no longer linked to you and fall under the same rule.
  • Guest sessions: deleted after 6 months without activity.
  • Friends and requests: until the friend is removed or the request is declined or cancelled.
  • Messages: until one of the two accounts is deleted, which deletes the whole conversation.
  • Server logs: a few days, as set by our providers.
  • Your emails to us: as long as needed to deal with your request.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You can change your username and email and delete your account yourself in Settings; for anything else, write to seblis@seznam.cz. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Czech supervisory authority: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (Office for Personal Data Protection), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.

8. Age

Wizard Chess is for people aged 15 and over. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we delete it.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and every change to accounts and games goes through our server, which checks it. Players can read only what they are allowed to.

10. Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We tell you about important changes in the game or by email before they apply.
