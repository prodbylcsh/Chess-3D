Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Wizard Chess collects, why, who helps us process it, and what rights you have. We keep it short and collect only what the game needs.

1. Who we are

Wizard Chess is run by Lukáš Schöbel, Czech Republic (“we”, “us”). He is the controller of your personal data. Contact: seblis@seznam.cz.

2. What we collect and why

Your account

When you sign up with email: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form), when you signed up and last signed in, and your interface language, so we can write our emails in your language. We use this to create and protect your account, sign you in and send you the emails the account needs (confirming your address, resetting your password, confirming an email change).
Legal basis: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google gives us your email address, name, profile picture link and a Google account ID. We use the email address for your account. The rest is kept with your sign-in record and is not shown in the game.
Legal basis: contract.

Your profile

Your username, profile icon, the date you joined, your progress through the first-time setup and when you last changed your username. Your username, icon and join date are visible to other players, and so are your rank, your game statistics and the items you use, which your device sends us, and whether you are online right now.
Legal basis: contract.

Friends and messages

Your friends and friend requests, and the messages you send and receive (text and game invitations), with when they were sent and read. Only you and the other player can see a conversation.
Legal basis: contract.

Online games

The games you play online: the moves, the result, the names shown at the board, the players’ account IDs and when the game was played. Anyone who has a game’s link can watch it.
Legal basis: contract.

Guests

If you play through an invite link without an account, we create an anonymous guest session (a random ID). We store only the name you enter and your games.
Legal basis: contract.

Technical data

Our providers’ servers record your IP address, browser and device information and the time of requests. This keeps the service running and secure (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts).
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you write to us

We use your message and email address to answer you.

3. What stays in your browser

The app keeps some data only in your browser’s storage: your sign-in session, your settings (language, sound, camera) and, for now, your coins, items, your rating and your game history. Of these, we receive only the snapshot that other players see (your rank, statistics and the items you use, see above). Clearing your browser data deletes the rest, and signing in on another device starts fresh there. We are moving this data to our servers and will update this policy before we do.

We don’t use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. The fonts and all other files of the game are served from our own site.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA): database, accounts and server functions. Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Resend (USA): sends our emails.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts the website and sees your IP address when you load it.
  • Google: only if you sign in with Google.

Supabase and Resend process data only on our behalf, under data processing terms. GitHub and Google handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies. We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are based in the USA. Transfers to them rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account removes them right away.
  • Online games: kept so that games can be resumed and looked back on. Games without a registered player are deleted after 6 months without activity. When you delete your account, your games are no longer linked to you and fall under the same rule.
  • Guest sessions: deleted after 6 months without activity.
  • Friends and requests: until the friend is removed or the request is declined or cancelled.
  • Messages: until one of the two accounts is deleted, which deletes the whole conversation.
  • Server logs: a few days, as set by our providers.
  • Your emails to us: as long as needed to deal with your request.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You can change your username and email and delete your account yourself in Settings; for anything else, write to seblis@seznam.cz. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Czech supervisory authority: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (Office for Personal Data Protection), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.

8. Age

Wizard Chess is for people aged 15 and over. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we delete it.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and every change to accounts and games goes through our server, which checks it. Players can read only what they are allowed to.

10. Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We tell you about important changes in the game or by email before they apply.
