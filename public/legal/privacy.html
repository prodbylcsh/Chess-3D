Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Wizard Chess collects, why, who helps us process it, and what rights you have. We keep it short and collect only what the game needs.

1. Who we are

Wizard Chess is run by Lukáš Schöbel, Czech Republic (“we”, “us”). He is the controller of your personal data. Contact: seblis@seznam.cz.

2. What we collect and why

Your account

When you sign up with email: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form), when you signed up and last signed in, and your interface language, so we can write our emails in your language. We use this to create and protect your account, sign you in and send you the emails the account needs (confirming your address, resetting your password, confirming an email change).
Legal basis: performing our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google gives us your email address, name, profile picture link and a Google account ID. We use the email address for your account. The rest is kept with your sign-in record and is not shown in the game.
Legal basis: contract.

Your profile

Your username, profile icon, the date you joined, your progress through the first-time setup and when you last changed your username, and who may watch your games (everyone with the link, your friends or nobody). Your username, icon and join date are visible to other players, and so are your rank, your game statistics, the awards you have earned (with when and the game that earned them), the items you use and whether you are online right now.
Legal basis: contract.

Friends and messages

Your friends and friend requests, and the messages you send and receive (text and game invitations), with when they were sent and read. Only you and the other player can see a conversation.
Legal basis: contract.

Blocks and reports

The players you block, and when. Only you see them; the blocked player is not told. When you report a player, we store who reported whom, the reason, your note and, for a reported message, a copy of that message, so that we can review it. The reported player does not see who reported them.
Legal basis: contract (blocks); our legitimate interest in a safe, fair service (reports).

Online games

The games you play online: the moves, the clocks (in timed games also how long each move took), the result, the names shown at the board, the players’ account IDs and when the game was played.
While a game runs, other players and visitors can watch it as both players’ settings allow (Settings → Who can watch my games: everyone with the game’s link, which is the default, only your friends, or nobody). Players you have blocked, or who have blocked you, never can. Spectators see the moves, the clocks, the result, both players’ names, icons, ranks and items, and how many people are watching; in ranked and coin games they see the moves two moves late while the game runs. Players who may watch your game also see that you are “playing now”. For this we keep a copy of each game for spectators, with the moves they may see.
Legal basis: contract.

Ranked, casual and coin games, coins and items

Your rating (MMR), coins, the items you own and use (for each item: how you got it, for how many coins, and when), your game statistics and streaks, and your season progress. A record of every change to your coins (games, stakes, payouts, purchases, season rewards, puzzle rewards and module unlocks). For each ranked, casual or coin game, a result: the opponent, the outcome and how the game ended, the number of moves, and the rating and coins it brought you. After a ranked, casual or coin game, the analysis your browser sends us: a score and the engine’s preferred move for every position of the game, worked out on your device by the Stockfish chess engine. We compare it with your opponent’s analysis and, when they match, store your accuracy with the result and, in ranked and casual games, adjust that game’s rating and coins by it (a coin game pays only its stake). While you search for a game, we keep your search (mode, time control, stake and rating) to find you an opponent, and delete it when a game is found or you stop searching.
Your exact rating, your coins and what each game brought you are visible only to you. Other signed-in players can see your recent results (opponent, outcome, how the game ended, number of moves, accuracy). Your username, icon and rank at the time appear in your opponents’ results.
Legal basis: contract.

Fair play

To keep ranked, casual and coin games fair, we check them for help from a chess engine. For each of these games we keep how long each of your moves took (measured by our server) and, from the analyses described above, the engine’s preferred move in every position. Your browser also measures, during each of your moves, how long the game was in the background or out of focus (another tab, app or window) and sends this with its analysis. From this we work out, for each game and over your recent games, how often you played the engine’s preferred move, how you spent your time, and how often you moved after leaving the game, and compare this with what is usual for players of your rank. When these numbers look unusual, the player is shown to us for a review. Nothing is decided automatically: a person (the operator) looks at the games before any step under the Terms (§4). Only we see these data.
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in fair games for all players. You can object (§7).

Puzzles

For each puzzle you solve or whose solution you look at: the module, the puzzle, your best stars (or that you saw the solution), when you first solved it and when you last finished it (this decides when your stars can improve in a review). For each module: when you unlocked it, when you finished it and the reward you got. While a puzzle is open, which puzzle it is and when you opened it (replaced by the next one you open). For the daily puzzle: the days you solved it with your stars and coins, and your streak (the days in a row, your best streak and the last day). Only you can see your puzzle progress.
Legal basis: contract.

Lessons

The lessons of Learn you have finished and when, the chapters whose reward you got, and whether you unlocked the paid chapters. Only you can see them.
Legal basis: contract.

Coin purchases

When you buy coins: for each purchase, the Paddle transaction number, the pack, the coins, the amount and currency you paid, and whether it was paid or refunded, with the dates. Paddle, which sells the coins (see below), asks for your email address, country and payment details; we never see your card or bank details.
Legal basis: contract; keeping records of purchases is also our legitimate interest in handling refunds and disputes.

Premium (subscription)

When you subscribe: your plan, billing period (monthly or yearly) and status (free trial, active, payment overdue, cancelled), the current period and when a cancellation takes effect, Paddle’s subscription and customer IDs, each paid period with its Paddle transaction number, amount and dates, and the coins and subscriber icons each month gave you. Also your streak shields and which games you opened in Game Review on each day (to count the day’s reviews). Others see only the Premium badge on your profile. Game Review itself runs in your browser: the analysis is not sent to us.
Legal basis: contract; keeping records of payments is also our legitimate interest in handling refunds and disputes.

Guests

If you play through an invite link or try puzzles or lessons without an account, we create an anonymous guest session (a random ID). We store only the name you enter, your games, the puzzles you solve with their stars, your daily-puzzle streak, the lessons you finish and the coins they earned. When you create an account or sign in on the same device, the puzzles, lessons and coins move into that account and the guest’s copy is deleted.
Legal basis: contract.

Technical data

Our providers’ servers record your IP address, browser and device information and the time of requests. This keeps the service running and secure (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts).
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you write to us

We use your message and email address to answer you.

3. What stays in your browser

The app keeps in your browser’s storage your sign-in session, your settings (language, sound, vibration, camera, time control), the name you last used as a guest, your guest session (so that your puzzles can move into an account you create or sign in to later), a copy of your profile so it shows quickly, and the moves of your last ranked, casual or coin games (with how long you were away from each game during your moves) until your browser has analysed them (at most a day). Games against the computer and on one device are played only in your browser and are not sent to us.

Since 11 October 2026, your coins, items, rating and game history are kept on our servers (see above). Everyone started fresh then: what earlier versions of the app kept in browsers was not carried over, and we never received it.

We don’t use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. The fonts and the game’s files, including the Stockfish chess engine that analyses your games and explains wrong moves in puzzles on your device, are served from our own site; the 3D models and pictures of shop items come from our storage at Cloudflare (see below). Only when you open the payment window to buy coins does your browser load Paddle’s checkout from Paddle’s servers.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA): database, accounts and server functions. Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Resend (USA): sends our emails.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts the website and sees your IP address when you load it.
  • Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare, Inc., USA): stores the 3D models and pictures of shop items and delivers them; sees your IP address when your browser loads them.
  • Google: only if you sign in with Google.
  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited, United Kingdom): sells the coins and the Premium subscription as our reseller and takes the payments. Paddle receives your account ID and the pack or plan from us, and everything you enter in its checkout, and handles it as an independent controller under its privacy policy.

Supabase, Resend and Cloudflare process data only on our behalf, under data processing terms. GitHub, Google and Paddle handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies. We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are based in the USA. Transfers to them rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Paddle is in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises as protecting personal data adequately.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account. Settings → Delete account removes them right away.
  • Coins, items, rating, statistics, game results and the coin record: until you delete your account, which deletes them right away. Your name in your opponents’ results stays as part of their history.
  • Puzzle progress and rewards: until you delete your account, which deletes them right away.
  • Coin purchases: until you delete your account. Paddle keeps its own records of the payment as the law requires of a seller.
  • Subscriptions and their payments: until you delete your account (which also ends a running subscription). Paddle keeps its own records as the law requires of a seller.
  • Game Review counts: a week.
  • Searches for a game: until a game is found or you stop searching.
  • Game analyses (the scores per position): two days; the accuracy stays with the result.
  • Fair play: what each game showed (your move times, how often you played the engine’s move or moved after leaving the game) for 12 months; the overall assessment and our review of it until you delete your account, which deletes both right away. The move times are also kept with the online game (see Online games).
  • Online games: kept so that games can be resumed and looked back on. Games without a registered player are deleted after 6 months without activity. When you delete your account, your games are no longer linked to you and fall under the same rule. The spectators’ copy of a game is deleted with the game.
  • Guest sessions: deleted after 6 months without activity (no game move and no puzzle), with their puzzles and coins.
  • Friends and requests: until the friend is removed or the request is declined or cancelled.
  • Blocks: until you unblock the player or one of the two accounts is deleted.
  • Reports: 12 months. A report stays when the reporter deletes their account (without the link to them), and goes when the reported account is deleted.
  • Messages: until one of the two accounts is deleted, which deletes the whole conversation.
  • Server logs: a few days, as set by our providers.
  • Your emails to us: as long as needed to deal with your request.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You can change your username and email and delete your account yourself in Settings; for anything else, write to seblis@seznam.cz. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Czech supervisory authority: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (Office for Personal Data Protection), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz.

8. Age

Wizard Chess is for people aged 15 and over. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we delete it.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and every change to accounts and games goes through our server, which checks it. Players can read only what they are allowed to.

10. Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. We tell you about important changes in the game or by email before they apply.
